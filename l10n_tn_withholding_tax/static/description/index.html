Withholding Tax

This module adds Tunisian withholding tax (retenue à la source) handling on bill payments.

It does not create any journal or rule of its own. Instead, it lets you flag bank journals as withholding tax journals, each with its own rate, and define Withholding Rules that link products to one of these journals, or apply based only on a minimum invoice total when no products are selected.

When you register a payment on a bill with an applicable rule, the payment wizard:

  • shows the applicable withholding taxes and the amount to withhold for each one;
  • hides the withholding tax journals that do not apply to the bill;
  • fills in the withholding amount when you select one of the applicable journals.

The withholding amount is computed on the bill total, taxes included, and is recorded as a payment in the withholding tax journal. Once a withholding tax has been paid on a bill, it is no longer proposed for that bill.

Installation

This addon is incompatible with Odoo’s native Withholding Tax on Payment (l10n_account_withholding_tax). Only one of these addons can be installed in a database, including databases with multiple companies. Odoo blocks installation when the other addon is already installed.

Configuration

This module does not create any journal or rule. You set them up in two steps.

Withholding tax journals

  1. Go to Accounting > Configuration > Journals.
  2. Create (or open) a journal of type Bank. Use one journal per withholding tax rate.
  3. Open the Advanced Settings tab and, in the Withholding Tax section, tick Journal includes Withholding Tax.
  4. Set the Rate of the withholding tax.

The Withholding Tax section is only shown on journals of type Bank.

Withholding rules

  1. Go to Accounting > Configuration > Withholding Rules.
  2. Create a rule and fill in:
    • Article: the products the rule applies to. Leave empty to apply the rule to any invoice meeting the minimum amount.
    • Description: a short explanation of the rule.
    • Document Type: Customer Invoice (out_invoice) or Vendor Bill (in_invoice). Leave empty to apply the rule to both.
    • Amount: the minimum bill total (taxes included) from which the withholding applies. The threshold is inclusive. Use 0 for no minimum. This amount cannot be negative.
    • Withholding Tax Journal: the journal holding the rate to apply. Only journals with Journal includes Withholding Tax ticked can be selected.

Accounting reference

Configure the withholding journal’s manual payment method with an explicit tax account in Outstanding Receipts/Payments Account:

Withholding flow Payment method Tax account type Withholding entry
Customer invoice Inbound Current Assets (asset_current) Debit tax receivable, credit the invoice’s customer account
Vendor bill Outbound Current Liabilities (liability_current) Debit the invoice’s supplier account, credit tax payable

Use dedicated reconcilable tax accounts, separate from the customer/supplier accounts and bank accounts. Configure account numbers according to the company’s chart of accounts; the module does not prescribe them. The journal’s default bank account is not the tax account: the withholding payment must use the account configured on its payment method. Configure separate journals for customer and supplier flows, even though the current version does not yet enforce journal direction.

For a gross invoice of 2,000 and a withholding of 30, register a withholding payment of 30 and a bank payment of 1,970. Reconcile both payment counterpart lines with the invoice. The invoice total stays 2,000 and its residual becomes zero; only the net payment affects the bank or its normal outstanding account.

Certificate confirmation changes the workflow and records the user and date. It must not change accounts, debit/credit amounts, residuals or reconciliations, or create another journal entry. The tax receivable/payable remains open after confirmation; its subsequent tax settlement is a separate operation.

Usage

Once the journals and rules are configured (see Configuration), withholding tax is handled from the payment wizard.

Withholding applies only when both the invoice company and invoice partner have Tunisia as their country. A missing country makes the document ineligible. Withholding journals and alerts are not offered for ineligible documents. Direct payment creation and confirmation enforce the same country restriction.

One receipt for several invoices

Select several invoices for the same customer and click Pay. Enable Withholding Tax Applicable, then choose Keep Unpaid or Register Withholding Payment.

The checkbox is shown only when several invoices are selected and at least one has an applicable withholding rule. It is hidden when none of the selected invoices meets the Tunisia country restriction or matches a withholding rule.

The allocation table shows each invoice’s remaining balance, applicable withholding journals, calculated withholding amount, and cash allocation. Different invoices can use different journals or several journals. Edit the selected journals and cash allocations as needed; withholding amounts are calculated from each journal’s rate and the invoice’s original total, rounded per journal in the invoice currency.

Changing the bank/cash amount fills each invoice’s balance after withholding in allocation order before moving to the next invoice. Once the cash is exhausted, later invoices receive no cash allocation. Each invoice’s withholding stays reserved. Editing cash allocations directly updates the bank/cash total while preserving the manual distribution. An amount above the total net balance keeps the existing allocations and displays a warning; it cannot consume withholding.

Exactly one bank/cash payment is created for the amount received. Its reconciliation follows the cash allocation of each invoice:

  • Keep Unpaid leaves the withholding amount outstanding on each invoice. No withholding payment is created.
  • Register Withholding Payment additionally creates one pending withholding payment per invoice/journal. Certificate confirmation remains a separate step.

A warning shows differences from the suggested net total. Partial payments are allowed when the edited cash allocations add up to the bank/cash amount. Confirmation is blocked for incompatible journals, stale balances, negative or excess allocations, or cash totals that do not match the payment. Differences are never written off automatically.

Allocation mode supports posted customer invoices or vendor bills in the company currency, for one partner, company and receivable/payable account. Credit notes and foreign currencies use the standard payment flow. Existing withholding payments are excluded from the suggested withholding journals to avoid duplicates.

Individual withholding payments

  1. Open a posted vendor bill that meets a withholding rule’s conditions, and click Pay.
  2. At the top of the wizard, a message lists the applicable withholding taxes, with the journal and the amount to withhold for each one.
  3. In Journal, select the withholding tax journal. The Amount is filled in automatically with the withholding amount.
  4. Click Create Payment.
  5. Pay the rest of the bill as usual with your bank journal.

A rule applies when the bill total is equal to or higher than the rule’s Amount and, if products are selected, the bill contains one of them. Rules without products also apply to invoice lines without a product. Amount-only and matching product rules can both apply. Withholding tax journals that do not match an applicable rule are not offered in the Journal field.

Once the withholding tax has been paid, its journal is no longer offered for that bill and the rule no longer appears in the wizard.

Invoice report with payments

When printing an invoice with payments, withholding payments appear first, labelled with their journal name (for example RS 1.5%), with the reconciled amount in the invoice currency. Other payments keep their usual labels, dates and relative order. The invoice balance and accounting entries are unchanged.

Certificate confirmation

Withholding payments remain In Process until the certificate is confirmed. The invoice or bill balance is reduced immediately, but a fully settled invoice or bill remains In Payment while its withholding certificate is pending.

  • Open Customers > Incoming Withholding Payments to track received withholding. Open a payment and click Validate Reception after receiving its certificate.
  • Open Vendors > Outgoing Withholding Payments to track issued withholding. Open a payment and click Confirm Certificate Delivery after delivering its certificate.

Both menus offer kanban and list views, with filters by state, partner, journal, date and company. Confirmation records the accounting user and date, marks the payment Paid, and updates the invoice or bill when all payments are matched. Confirmation does not create another journal entry. Resetting, canceling or rejecting a payment clears its confirmation. Pending withholding payments still prevent duplicate registration; canceled payments no longer block the rule.

On upgrade, existing Paid withholding payments are treated as confirmed. Their confirmation user and date stay empty because no historical certificate confirmation was recorded.

Customer bills of exchange, using native payments, journal entries and
reconciliation. A received bill covers an invoice without pretending
that cash has already been collected.

Configuration
=============

The Tunisian chart template creates the bank-type journal TRT and its
inbound Traite payment method. The method uses the chart's
bills-receivable account (reference l10n_tn_413). The technical journal
is hidden from the bank dashboard.

For an existing Tunisian company, open the company's Traites tunisiennes
page and use Ajouter le journal Traites depuis le plan comptable. This
adds missing bill records only. It does not reload accounts or overwrite
an existing journal's customized name.

Validate the accounts with the company's accountant. The bills account
must be receivable, reconcilable, different from the customer account
and different from the journal's default bank account. Actual bank/cash
receipts use the method's configured outstanding account when present.
Otherwise, Odoo resolves its default receipts account, falling back to
the company's transfer account. An accounting entry is required to
settle the bill, and the resolved collection account must differ from
the bill counterpart account. A cash method can use the cash account
directly.

Set a follow-up manager, reminder lead time and escalation delay on the
company. Daily activities stay internal: no automatic email or reminder
is sent to the debtor.

Usage
=====

- Register a payment using Traite from one or more posted invoices for a
  single customer, receivable account, company and TND currency.
  Selecting Traite groups compatible invoices into one payment
  automatically, allowing a partial amount. Supply the bill reference,
  maturity and signed acceptance.
- Alternatively, prepare a draft bill under Accounting / Traites clients
  / Portefeuille et suivi. Select the invoices, attach the signed title
  and confirm receipt when acceptance is evidenced. Printing a draft
  does not record payment.
- The invoice's residual decreases through native reconciliation. A
  partially covered invoice remains Partially Paid; a fully covered bill
  still awaiting collection stays In Payment, including in the Community
  accounting workflow.
- Record collections from the bill. A new native payment credits the
  bills account directly. Payments using the bank/cash account directly
  settle immediately. An outstanding payment reserves principal until
  the user confirms receipt from the collection, with or without bank
  matching. This confirms the native payment and settles the bill
  without requiring a bank statement. The outstanding account remains
  open until bank matching if that workflow is used. An existing payment
  or bank-transaction credit can also be used, provided its remaining
  credit exactly matches the selected principal.
- Declare an unpaid principal with a reason and an optional supporting
  document. The original invoices reopen for the returned amount while
  collected fractions remain paid. The corresponding incident remains
  available for explicit recovery allocation.
- Use Recouvrer on the incident for later collections. Ordinary invoice
  payments may cover unrelated open balances, but cannot silently
  consume principal reserved for incident follow-up.
- A replacement bill can cover the exact outstanding, unreserved
  incidents of a rejected bill. Link the previous bill and the original
  invoices. Transfers to the replacement are shown separately from cash
  recoveries; canceling the replacement restores the old incident
  balance.
- Collection reversal and bill cancellation use reversing entries.
  Posted bill details and the original invoice links are protected
  against ordinary edits.

Dashboard and follow-up
-----------------------

The dashboard is a live native spreadsheet with five sheets:

- Synthèse: current principal, portfolio, bill exposure, overdue titles,
  pending receipts, unpaid balances and actionable dossiers.
- Échéancier: expected principal by month and exclusive delay buckets.
- Portefeuille: customer/custody and responsible analyses.
- Impayés et recouvrement: current incident balances, period incident
  flows and linked dossiers.
- Encaissements et recouvrements: confirmed period collections and
  recoveries.

Filter by company, customer, responsible, maturity and flow period. The
flow period does not filter current-stock cards. Amounts awaiting
confirmation are already included in active principal; never add them a
second time. Pivot totals cover all records; detailed spreadsheet lists
show the first 50 dossiers. Use the native list for the full work queue
and pivot source drill-down.

The native work queue exposes next action, due date, last contact,
promise, responsible and financial balances. Promises never change
accounting totals. Daily activities cover approaching maturity, overdue
confirmation, incidents, pending collections and expired promises, with
escalation and no duplicate automated activity for the same bill.

The load timestamp is refreshed when the dashboard is loaded.
Refresh/reload the spreadsheet after operations. Source queries use the
viewer's accounting rights and allowed companies; no sample business
values are embedded in the spreadsheet. Public snapshot sharing is
disabled for this dashboard because a public cached snapshot cannot
preserve each viewer's accounting permissions.

Implementation
--------------

Receipt checks use the native journal-entry generation hook;
reconciliation and sharing rules use ORM constraints. The two remaining
write guards protect confirmed payment details and posted financial
lines.

Settlements and incidents have read-only ACLs. Authorized workflow
actions perform financial operations with the caller's accounting
permissions, then use narrowly scoped elevated access to append or
update history and its proof. Direct creation and editing of history
remain unavailable to accounting users. Each history model has its own
security XML file with company rules.

Concurrency protection uses Odoo 19's native lock_for_update API. The
module contains no handwritten SQL queries. A field restricted to
technical users records whether a transient operation has already been
applied.

Limits and acceptance
---------------------

The initial scope is customer bills in TND with fixed maturity,
including partial collections. Supplier bills, discounting,
endorsements, guarantees, partial legal acceptance, grouped remittance
files, foreign currencies and legal deadline automation are outside this
version.

The PDF is a proposed document layout to validate against the client's
actual title. A generated PDF or an Odoo confirmation is not a legal
signature. No bill stamp tax is generated automatically.

Stamp-duty rules and compatibility with invoice-stamp addons belong to
those addons. This module does not depend on or override their tax
logic.

Bank remittance actions and fee management are outside the supported
workflow.

No arbitrary historical portfolio reconstruction or write-off automation
is included. Incident closure is driven by explicit collections or
replacement; it is never a manual financial-status change.

The source includes business tests for coverage, partial settlement,
pending confirmation, rejection, recovery, reversal, replacement, chart
setup, workflow guards and dashboard access. Odoo installation and test
execution require an explicitly authorized test database; static
validation alone does not establish accounting acceptance.

Shared eligibility checks for Tunisian accounting addons.

A transaction is domestic when both the document's company and invoice
partner have Tunisia as their country. Missing countries are treated as
ineligible. The active company in the user's session does not affect
this check.

The account.move method ``_l10n_tn_is_domestic_transaction`` provides
the common check. The computed field ``l10n_tn_is_domestic_transaction``
exposes it to views. Stamp Tax, Tax Exemption and Withholding Tax retain
their own applicability rules and use this shared check.

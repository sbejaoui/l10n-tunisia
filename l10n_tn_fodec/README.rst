Adds FODEC tax definitions to the standard Tunisian chart of accounts
for new companies and, on installation, existing companies using the
``tn`` chart.

Configuration
=============

- Sales FODEC: 1% of the untaxed line amount, posted to the existing
  account 43658 (Other turnover taxes).
- Purchase FODEC: 1%, charged to the invoice line's account as a
  non-recoverable cost, without posting it to a deductible VAT account.
- Both taxes are excluded from the price, have sequence 0 (before the
  standard VAT taxes), ignore preceding taxes, and increase the base of
  subsequent taxes. VAT must retain "Base Affected by Previous Taxes"
  and a higher sequence.
- The export fiscal position replaces sales FODEC with a separate 0%
  FODEC tax. A VAT exemption alone does not establish a FODEC exemption;
  no automatic mapping is added to the generic Exemption fiscal
  position.

Select FODEC alongside VAT only for supplies subject to FODEC. This
addon does not classify products, change company default taxes, or apply
taxes automatically to every transaction. For imports, calculate FODEC
on the customs value; an ordinary foreign supplier bill is not a customs
assessment.

For 100.000 TND excluding tax, FODEC is 1.000 TND and 19% VAT on 101.000
TND is 19.190 TND, giving 120.190 TND before stamp duty.

Existing companies receive these definitions when the addon is
installed. Future chart loads and reloads use the standard Odoo
chart-template mechanism. FODEC has its own tax group; this addon does
not extend the statutory tax report or assign VAT report tags to FODEC.

Legal references
================

- Articles 36 and 37 of law 99-101 of 31 December 1999, as amended,
  define scope, the 1% rate and the export exemption. Decree 2000-634,
  amended by decrees 2008-4111 and 2012-435, lists covered products.
  Applicability depends on the supply and the taxpayer's role, not
  merely the company's industry. See the Ministry of Finance
  compilation, pages 134-137:
  https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/RECUEIL%20DES%20TEXTES%20RELATIFS%20AUX%20DROITS%20ET%20TAXES%20NON%20INCORPORES%20DANS%20LES%20CODES%20FISCAUX%202017.pdf
- The Ministry of Finance confirms that FODEC forms part of the VAT
  base: https://www.finances.gov.tn/fr/faq?page=8

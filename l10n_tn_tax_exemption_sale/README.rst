Sales orders normally copy their fiscal position to the invoice. This
module recomputes it for sales invoices and credit notes linked to sales
order lines in companies using the Tunisian chart of accounts.

The invoice uses the exemption attestation valid for its partner and
invoice date. If the date is empty, the tax exemption module uses
today's date. When the fiscal position changes, Odoo updates the
invoice's taxes and accounts. The sales order keeps its original fiscal
position and taxes.

This module installs automatically when **Sales** and **Tax Exemption
for Tunisia** are installed.

Usage
=====

Create an invoice from a confirmed sales order. The invoice's fiscal
position is recomputed using the tax exemption module, even when the
order specifies a different fiscal position.

Check the invoice date, selected attestation, fiscal position, and taxes
before posting. A valid attestation applies its fiscal position; without
one, the customer's accounting fiscal position is used.
